My Role & Approach

I supported Society Socks' organic growth strategy by researching backlink opportunities, identifying relevant brand and creator partnerships, and conducting PR outreach. I used Ahrefs to assess SEO opportunities and monitor keyword rankings, referring domains and organic search performance. This work contributed to a 15% increase in organic search traffic and a 5–10% increase in referring domains.

How I Evaluated Growth Opportunities

1. SEO research

Keyword rankings, search visibility and backlink gaps.

2. Prospect evaluation

Domain authority, relevance and potential referral value.

3. Partnership outreach

Identifying brands, creators and publications for potential collaborations.

Prospect Scoring Rubric (Implemented in Ahrefs + Google Sheets CRM):

  • Domain Range & Authority: Filtered out spam PBNs and scraped directories; prioritized DR 40-75 editorial blogs, sustainable fashion journals, and university/corporate partner sites.
  • Keyword Ranking Overlap: Verified prospective sites ranked for relevant terms ("ethical clothing", "corporate holiday gifts", "best men's subscription boxes").
  • Link Profile Health: Inspected referring domain velocity to confirm prospective partners were not under algorithmic search penalties.
